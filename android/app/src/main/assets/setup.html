Connect to opencode

Enter the address of the opencode·pixel bridge running on your computer.

  1. On your computer: opencode serve and npm start in the opencode-pixel folder.
  2. Give it an HTTPS address: tailscale serve --bg 7878
  3. Paste that https://….ts.net address here. Tailscale must be on for this phone too.

A local address like http://192.168.1.20:7878 also works on your home Wi-Fi (start the bridge with OCB_HOST=0.0.0.0 and leave OCB_SECURE_COOKIES off).
